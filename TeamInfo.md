## Project Summary

FocusFeed is a personalized recommendation platform designed to bring the most relevant and engaging current events directly to users. The goal of this project is to be a space where all the news, updates, and discussions about a topic a person cares about is unified in one convenient place, our app. Initially a user will begin by selecting a few interests like technology, climate, sports, or global events and FocusFeed will use its recommendations algorithm, powered by RAG, in order to build a smart-queue of article compilations via dynamic feed tailored to those preferences, along with an intelligent interactive chatbot relevant to the news category. The user can then like or dislike articles and the algorithm will update the user’s feed on-demand.
